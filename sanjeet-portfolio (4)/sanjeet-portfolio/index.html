Read the case study

01Goal

One place where blood donors, hospitals and people who need blood can find each other, instead of relying on scattered phone calls and messages.

02Who it serves

Donors register their blood type, weight, city and availability. Hospitals post blood requests. Patients and families browse the donor directory and the live inventory.

03Key flows

  • Requests: three urgency levels, critical (within 2 hours), urgent (within 24 hours) and standard (2–5 days).
  • Inventory: live stock by blood type and hospital.
  • Directory: donors filtered by blood type.
  • Accounts: sign-up and login with OTP verification, plus a donor profile with alert preferences.

04Status

A working prototype hosted on GitHub Pages. It runs on demo data and a demo OTP, so it demonstrates the flows rather than serving real donors or hospitals.
